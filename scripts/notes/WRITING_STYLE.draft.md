## Voice
- First person, plain and specific. Says what he built, then what it doesn't do yet.
- Casual openers in his own words ("a question from myself :P", "Anyways", "funnily enough").
  The technical sections stay sober and exact.
- Self-aware and understated: no hype, no "game-changing". Happy to call generated text "AI-slop".
- Thanks the reader at the end of personal intros.

## Structure
- A short intro paragraph on why he cared, then `##` sections named for the decision
  ("Separating sampling, aggregation, and communications").
- Links to exact commits or files when he makes a claim about code.
- States limits outright: "It does not establish…", "remains platform work", "still to do".
- Short tables for formats; code identifiers in backticks.

## Don'ts
- No invented metrics, users or results.
- No medical, safety or driving claims.
- Nothing about private repos, school or job applications.
